Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive service requests?

Set your availability ON, keep profile updated, and requests will appear in Requests.

How do I reject a request?

Open Requests, enter a valid rejection reason, then reject the request.

When can I mark job completed?

Only after payment is received (online paid or cash marked paid).
