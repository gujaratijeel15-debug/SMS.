Terms & Conditions

By using SMS, providers agree to maintain accurate profile and service details, follow platform rules, and handle requests responsibly.

Rejections must include valid reasons, and completed jobs should follow payment confirmation rules. Policies may update for security and quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start receiving jobs?

Complete provider profile, choose your service, and set availability active.

Can I reject a request?

Yes. You can reject pending requests, but a valid rejection reason is required.

When can I complete a job?

After payment status is marked as paid, then you can complete the job.

How do reviews work?

Users can submit ratings and response after completed service delivery.
